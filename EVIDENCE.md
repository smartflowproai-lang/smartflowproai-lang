Three notes on reading this index. Links to GitHub artifacts point at content under version control; history is visible per file. Links to our own domain are self-hosted and therefore weaker evidence than the version-controlled entries — that asymmetry is why the SQL and artifacts live on GitHub. Nothing here requires an account, a payment, or our cooperation to verify.
